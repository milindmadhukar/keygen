# Case & Plate Requirements

> [!info] Running checklist
> Every PCB or electronics decision that the **case**, **switch plate** or **faceplate** must account for goes here as soon as it's made. Read this before designing either. Decisions themselves live in [[12 - final design]]; the case as designed is in [[09 - case design]].
>
> The positions table at the bottom is generated by `tools/case_refs.py` in the repo. Re-run it after any PCB change.

## Openings in the case

All the ports are on the **top edge** of each half, next to the controller, so one cut-out area per half covers them. The left and right halves mirror each other.

| Opening | Where | Size / notes |
|---|---|---|
| **USB-C** | Top wall, at the controller | The nice!nano (18 mm wide) hangs **under** the PCB on sockets, and its USB-C end reaches ~1 mm past the PCB edge; the edge is slanted, so one corner reaches ~1.6 mm. The case has an **open slot 20 mm wide** from the floor up to the PCB's underside, which clears the whole controller end and any cable overmould. |
| **Power switch** (MSK12C02, side-actuated) | Top wall, near the USB-C | The lever pokes out past the PCB edge, at the PCB's underside. A **9 × 3.4 mm slot** at the lever. The wall is thinned to **1.5 mm** from 10 mm before the USB-C to the corner past the switch, so a fingernail reaches the lever. |
| **Reset pinhole** | Case **floor**, under the reset button | The 6 × 6 mm, 5 mm-tall button sits on the PCB underside. You press it with a **paperclip** through a **Ø 2 mm hole**, countersunk outside, with a guide tube (Ø 5 mm) up to 1 mm under the button. ZMK can also enter the bootloader from a key combo. |
| **Rotary encoder knob** (both halves, mirrored) | Top: through the switch plate | The EC11 body is ~12 × 12 mm and 4.5 mm tall on the PCB top, so the plate has a **13 × 13 mm cut-out** around it; above it only the 7 mm bushing (to 11.5 mm) passes through a 9 mm hole in the top frame's deck, under the knob, whose underside is 2.9 mm above the deck. The knob is Ø 17 mm, its edge 4.5 mm below the OLED, 5.7 mm from the thumb cluster's top key and 9.1 mm from the G/H column. Shafts at (167.0, 129.0) left and (256.5, 129.0) right, in board coordinates (KiCad mm). |
| **OLED window** (both halves) | Top: through the switch plate, above the knob | The 27.5 × 28 mm SSD1306 module stands on its own header pins and 2.5 mm spacer, so its glass sits about level with the plate top (~5 mm above the PCB). The plate has a **28.5 × 29 mm window**, and the top frame's deck a window round the active area, opening towards the thumbs; the glass sits 3.5 mm below the deck top. Module at x 151–178.5, y 88–116 (left) and x 245–272.5, y 88–116 (right), in board coordinates, over the controller's antenna end. `tools/gen_case.py` checks the case against the modules. |

## Inside the case (under the PCB)

- **Tallest part: the controller on sockets.** About 4.5 mm of low-profile socket plus the nice!nano's 3.2 mm (mid-mount USB-C), so about **7.5 mm below the PCB**. Measure the real one before fixing the cavity depth.
- **Battery** (decided 2026-10-20, capacity raised 2026-10-22, bay 2026-10-22): LiPo pouch **≤ 5 mm thick, 2500–3000 mAh, at most 60 × 88 mm** including the protection board (e.g. 505080, 80 × 50 × 5 mm). It lies in a **bay sunk 1 mm into the case floor**, long side across the board, under the outer and middle key columns, held with double-sided tape. Measure the actual cell before printing.
  - Bay: the cell plus 1.5 mm each side, 63 × 91 mm. Cell position (same coordinates as the table below): left half x 16.7–104.7, y 6.4–66.4; right half x 57.9–145.9, y 6.4–66.4. `tools/case_geometry.py` places it, as near the battery connector as the rules below allow.
  - Keep it **≥ 15 mm from the controller's antenna end** (no foil near the antenna) and ≥ 3 mm from the controller, the JST-PH connector, the slide switch, the reset button, the encoder and the OLED header pins. It has 10 mm.
  - The lead end faces the side-entry JST-PH next to the controller, about 40 mm away, so a lead of 60 mm or more reaches it.
  - Height: bay floor 10.0 mm below the PCB's underside. A 5.5 mm cell (5 mm plus tolerance and swelling) leaves 2.5 mm to the sockets and diodes (2.0 mm under the PCB), and 1.0 mm with the floating stack pressed 1.5 mm down. `tools/gen_case.py` checks both. The battery adds no case height.
- **Case height:** 3.0 mm floor + 1.5 mm float gap + 7.5 mm controller + 1.6 mm PCB puts the PCB top 13.6 mm above the desk, the plate top 18.6 mm and keycap tops about 33 mm, before tenting and feet. The full stack is in [[09 - case design#The stack]].
- **Plate standoff screws** under the PCB (M2 button heads, 1.1 mm) are lower than the hotswap sockets.
- **Other underside parts.** Kailh hotswap sockets (~1.8 mm), through-hole diodes (~2 mm body, leads clipped flush), reverse-mount LEDs (nearly flush) and the switch pins poking through. Allow **≥ 4 mm** clear under the key area.
- **Bluetooth antenna.** The nRF52840's chip antenna is at the controller's **bottom end** (opposite USB-C). The case must be **plastic**, with **no metal plate, weight or screws** under or right next to that end. The PCB already keeps ground fill out of that area.

## Switch plate (faceplate)

- **FR-4, 1.6 mm**, black mask, no copper, made by the PCB fab from `case/plate/gerber/keygen-plate-{left,right}-gerbers.zip` (settings in `case/plate/PLATE.md`). MX clips are made for 1.5 mm and take 1.6 mm.
- **14.0 × 14.0 mm** square cut-out per switch, with 0.5 mm corner radii from the fab's router bit.
  - Thumb keys follow the PCB rotation (±15° / ±30°).
  - The four tall 1.5u thumb switches per half are turned 90°, but the cut-out is square so that doesn't matter.
- **No stabilizer cut-outs.** Nothing is wider than 1.5u.
- **Plate-to-PCB gap.** The plate top sits 5.0 mm above the PCB top (MX standard), so the gap is 3.4 mm. **M2 × 3.5 mm female-female standoffs** at all five holes per half, screwed from below with M2 × 4 screws.
- **Plate screws** (M2 × 4 button head, Ø 3.5 mm head) only where the head clears the switch top housings (15.6 mm square) by 0.3 mm: H102–H105 and H202–H204. At H101, H201 and H205 there is 1.65–1.98 mm of room, so those standoffs are screwed from below only and their top bears on the plate.
- **Mounting holes** line up with the PCB's M2 holes (table below), 2.2 mm.
- **Gasket tabs:** 8 per half, 12 × 5 mm, on the plate edge, kept 22 mm from the USB-C and the switch lever. The right half's are the left's, mirrored.
- **Encoder cut-out** (13 × 13 mm) and **OLED window** (28.5 × 29 mm) on both halves (see above).

## Lighting

- **Per-key LEDs** sit **south** of each switch (under the MX LED window), so shine-through legends would need south-facing keycaps.
- **Opaque floor.** All the lighting is per-key and shows through the keycaps, so the case floor needs no diffuser.

## Ergonomics and feel

- **Weight in the base.** Light halves slide when you press inward (the Wolf and Dactyl builders both said so). Add steel or washers, but **not under the antenna end**.
- **Tenting.**
  - Screw-in legs (Corne style: 18 mm front / 40 mm rear) or magnetic phone stands; not chosen yet. The case floor is flat until then.
  - The angled stand you wanted.
- **Rubber feet** and **foam layers** (case foam, PE switch foam) for a thocky sound. See [[07 - switch & keycap selection]].
- **Keycaps.** Blank DSA PBT, smoke translucent: 52 × 1u, 10 × 1.25u, 4 × 1.5u. Switches: Akko V3 Lavender Purple Pro.
