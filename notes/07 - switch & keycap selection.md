## Switches

### Akko V3 Lavender Purple Pro

| | |
|---|---|
| Type | Tactile, light, 5-pin MX |
| Force | 40 gf operating, 55 gf rounded bump |
| Travel | 3.8 mm |
| Housing | Clear PC top, nylon bottom, POM stem |
| Sound | Creamy and quiet |
| Quantity | 66 used, 24 spare (two packs of 45) |

**Why:** feedback on every press without a heavy bump, which suits small hands. The clear top lets the per-key LEDs through. The hotswap sockets let any switch be pulled and replaced without soldering, and the 24 spares cover that.

### Requirements it meets
- MX, in Kailh MX hotswap sockets
- Creamy rather than loud
- Light tactile

## Keycaps

### Blank DSA PBT, smoke translucent

- **Blank:** only the letters are fixed (QWERTY), so every other key can move between layers and positions freely.
- **Translucent:** the per-key RGB also shows status, and translucent caps show each key's colour across the whole cap.
- **DSA:** a uniform profile, so any cap works in any position, including the rotated thumb keys.
- **PBT:** textured, resists shine.

## Sound

The thocky, creamy sound comes from the switch and the build together:

- **Switch:** nylon bottom housing and a POM stem.
- **Keycaps:** thick PBT.
- **Foam:** a 0.5 mm Poron ring switch pad on the PCB under each switch, 2 mm EVA plate foam between the pads and the plate, and 5 mm EVA in the case floor. The plate floats on Poron gaskets. See [[09 - case design#Sound]].
