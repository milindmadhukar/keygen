# 3D model sources

- `SW_Cherry_MX_PCB.wrl`, `SW_Cherry_MX_PCB.stp` — from [kiswitch](https://github.com/kiswitch/kiswitch)
  (`library/3dmodels/3d-library.3dshapes`), dual licensed MIT / CC-BY-SA 4.0.
- `Keycap_*.wrl`, `Keycap_DSA_*.stl` — DSA keycaps generated by `tools/gen_keycaps.py` with
  [KeyV2](https://github.com/rsheldiii/KeyV2) (`lib/KeyV2`, GPL-3.0).
- `nRF52840_ProMicro.wrl` — generated by `tools/gen_schematic.py` (a simple shape for the 3D view, not
  manufacturing data).
- `Knob_17mm.step`, `OLED_096_I2C.step` — generated by `tools/gen_models.py` (the encoder knob and the
  OLED module, for the 3D view and renders).
