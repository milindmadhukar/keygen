# Ordering the Keygen switch plates

The plate is a plain FR-4 board with no copper. Place **two orders**, like the PCB:
`gerber/keygen-plate-left-gerbers.zip` and `gerber/keygen-plate-right-gerbers.zip`, 5 of each.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Layers | 2 (both copper layers are empty) |
| Material | FR-4, 1.6 mm |
| Board size | one half: 171.8 × 130.9 mm |
| Quantity | 5 left + 5 right (two orders) |
| Surface finish | HASL lead-free (no copper is exposed) |
| Soldermask / silkscreen | **black** both sides / none |
| Assembly | none |

**Things to tell the fab or check in their DFM report**
- It is a keyboard switch plate: **no copper** on either layer. Empty copper Gerbers are on purpose.
- 66 square switch cut-outs (33 per half), 14.0 × 14.0 mm with 0.5 mm corner radii, plus
  a 13 × 13 mm encoder cut-out and a 28.5 × 29 mm OLED window per half. All are internal Edge.Cuts outlines to be
  routed (milled) with a bit of 1.0 mm or less, so the corners stay at 0.5 mm.
- The outline has rectangular tabs (12 × 5 mm) on its edges. They are part of the board.
- Five 2.2 mm non-plated holes per half (M2).

Generated by `tools/gen_plate.py` from `pcb/keygen.kicad_pcb`.
Files in each zip: keygen-plate-right-B_Cu.gbl, keygen-plate-right-B_Mask.gbs, keygen-plate-right-B_Silkscreen.gbo, keygen-plate-right-Edge_Cuts.gm1, keygen-plate-right-F_Cu.gtl, keygen-plate-right-F_Mask.gts, keygen-plate-right-F_Silkscreen.gto, keygen-plate-right-NPTH-drl_map.gbr, keygen-plate-right-NPTH.drl, keygen-plate-right-PTH-drl_map.gbr, keygen-plate-right-PTH.drl, keygen-plate-right-job.gbrjob

## Cutting the plates from sheet (CNC router or laser)

`dxf/keygen-plate-left.dxf` and `dxf/keygen-plate-right.dxf` are the same plates as 1:1 DXFs in mm,
seen from above, one closed outline per contour, on three layers:

| Layer | Cut |
|---|---|
| `CUTOUTS` | inside the line (pockets through): switch, encoder and OLED cut-outs |
| `OUTLINE` | outside the line: the plate edge with its gasket tabs |
| `DRILL` | 2.2 mm holes, drilled (smaller than a router bit) |

- Sheet: 1.6 mm (1.5 mm also fits; the MX clips are made for it), at least
  180 × 139 mm per half.
- The lines are the finished size. The cutter offset (tool radius, or the laser's kerf) is set in
  the shop's CAM, not drawn in: the 14.0 mm switch cut-outs must come out 14.0 mm, to about 0.05 mm.
- Each cut-out corner has a dogbone (a half circle through the corner) for a router bit of
  3.175 mm (1/8 in.) or smaller, so the switches sit square. A laser cuts them as drawn.
- Cut `dxf/keygen-plate-fit-test.dxf` first: five cut-outs from 13.90 to 14.10 mm, the
  first marked by one hole below it, the second by two, and so on. A switch should press in with
  a click and not rattle in the 14.00 mm one (three holes); if a neighbour fits better, change the
  offset by the difference before cutting the plates.
