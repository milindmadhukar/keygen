# Electrical and lighting logic audit

Checked 2026-10-28, and again on 2026-11-09 after the LED supply became a 5 V boost, against the routed `pcb/keygen.kicad_pcb`, both schematics, the pinned ZMK
source in `firmware/west.yml`, the component datasheets in `docs/datasheets/`, and the mechanical
model. This is a design review; brightness, battery current, RF range, and temperature still need a
powered prototype.

## What checks out

| Area | Result |
|---|---|

| Key matrix | 33 keys and one encoder press per half, 5 rows × 7 columns. Each switch connects to a column and a diode anode; every diode cathode reaches its intended row. The `col2row` firmware direction matches. |

| Battery switch and LED boost | The slide switch carries gate current only; Q101/Q201 carries the battery path. U102/U202 (TPS61023) boost B+ to 0.595 V × (1 + 732k/100k) = 4.95 V (4.83–5.08 V over the reference tolerance), inside the SK6812MINI-E's 3.7–5.5 V for any cell voltage. P0.08 drives the boost's enable directly (≥ 2.9 V against a 1.2 V threshold) and the 100 kΩ R103/R203 holds it off before firmware takes over; shut down, the converter disconnects its output (0.1 µA). The inductor, output and input capacitors sit within 7 mm of the IC, laid out as the datasheet's example. The JST-PH connector is rated 2 A with AWG 24 wire, above the estimated capped LED load; the actual pack wiring and protection board rating are not specified. |
| LED chain | Each half has 33 LEDs in order. All VDD pads reach LED_VDD and all VSS pads reach GND. Every LED has a 100 nF footprint beside it, every fourth fitted, and a 10 µF capacitor sits beside the last LED. ## Limits that DRC cannot clear

1. **Full-strip brightness can outrun charging.** The datasheet gives about 12 mA per colour at 5 V and 1 mA static per LED. At the firmware's 40% PWM cap, all-white load is approximately `33 × (1 + 0.4 × 36) = 508 mA` per half at 5 V, about 740 mA from a 3.8 V cell through the boost at 90 % efficiency. A single saturated colour is about 191 mA at 5 V, about 280 mA from the cell. With the BOOST link bridged the SuperMini charges at about 300 mA, so white lighting can still discharge the cell while USB is connected, and the battery level reads full on USB. The boost's 2.7 A minimum switch limit and the AO3401A are ample; the battery pack and protection rating have not been established.
2. **The battery-lighting policy needs a powered test.** The Keygen module (`firmware/module/src/power/battery_lighting.c`) replaces ZMK's USB auto-off: on battery the lighting stays off unless `&kg_bl` allows it, is capped at half the brightness scale, halved below 20 % charge and off below 10 %; whenever the lighting is off it disables ext-power, so the boost and the rail are off. It re-checks after every USB, battery and activity event because ZMK's idle wake can restore the lighting. Measure LED_VDD and battery current after a battery-only boot, USB removal, idle and wake, with lighting on battery allowed and not.
3. **No battery, no lighting.** With no cell fitted, the controller, OLED and Bluetooth run from USB, but B+ is only the charger's output, so the boost has no proper supply. Keep a cell fitted.
4. **The boost's layout and the LED rail need a scope.** DRC cannot judge the switch node's ringing or the rail's ripple under PWM. Check SW and LED_VDD at the first and last LEDs at full white, and that the 0.5 mm-pitch SOT-563 has no bridges.
5. **Optical brightness cannot be signed off from CAD.** The opening and switch positions are correct, but the chosen smoke translucent PBT caps and actual Akko switch housing determine how much light reaches the top. The 40% cap limits output. The pinned ZMK build starts with `BRT_START=40` and then scales that by `BRT_MAX=40`, so a fresh build initially sends about 16% PWM, even though the maximum reachable with the brightness keys is 40%. Test one fully assembled switch/cap, then all 33 at full white and at the default setting; measure the farthest LED_VDD point and battery current. The renders are illustrative, not brightness evidence.
6. **Decoupling is partly fitted.** Page 9 of the [SK6812MINI-E datasheet](datasheets/README.md) shows a capacitor at every pixel and a resistor on the data input. Every LED has a footprint, every fourth is fitted, and R105/R205 sits at the first DIN. If a full-white step corrupts the chain, fit the optional capacitors.
7. **RF and assembly checks remain.** Ground pour is kept out from under each controller's assumed antenna tip, but 60 left and 39 right trace segments cross that region. Test wireless range with the lighting on and off: the boost switches at 1 MHz about 15 mm from the antenna end. Check the purchased battery connector polarity and OLED header order before applying power. With the battery switch off and USB present, the charger can conduct through Q101/Q201's body diode, so leave the switch on when charging and verify charge termination on the actual controller.

## Bring-up acceptance measurements

- With a protected, correctly polarised cell and USB disconnected, check VBAT, VCC and LED_VDD at boot, after idle, and in sleep. LED_VDD must be near zero whenever lighting is off.
- With USB on each half, enable full white at the 40% cap and measure cell current, LED_VDD at the first and last pixels, and the boost's and Q101/Q201's temperature. Repeat on battery near 3.5 V with lighting on battery allowed. Confirm the protection board and lead can carry the measured current.
- Look through the real switch and cap at several colours, then verify every LED and both encoders, keys, displays and Bluetooth link. Do not infer visible brightness from the CAD render.
