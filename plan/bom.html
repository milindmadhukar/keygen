Bill of materials

Everything needed for the keyboard, with spares, and where to get it in India or from abroad. Prices are what the listings showed on 20–22 Oct 2026, or rough guides where marked with ~. Back to the build plan.

Buying strategy: one AliExpress order, shipped to Australia and brought to India by hand, for the SK6812MINI-E LEDs (not stocked in India), the controllers, the knobs, the switch pads, the gaskets and the keycaps; BatteriesShop.in for the LiPo cells once pack protection, connector and dimensions are confirmed; StacksKB for the switches, diodes and puller, Robu.in for the encoders, small parts and the case print, Amazon.in for the polycarbonate sheet, the EVA foam, the feet, flux and Kapton tape, and JLCPCB for the boards.
