Bill of materials

Everything needed for the keyboard, with spares, and where to get it in India or from abroad. Prices are what the listings showed on 20 Oct 2026, or rough guides where marked with ~. Back to the build plan.

Buying strategy: one LCSC or AliExpress order for the SK6812MINI-E LEDs (not stocked in India) and the 1.25u and 1.5u keycaps, StacksKB for switches and diodes, Meckeys for the 1u keycaps, Robu.in or Lamington Road for batteries and small parts, and Lion Circuits (Bengaluru) for the boards. Batteries rarely ship to India from abroad, so buy those locally.
