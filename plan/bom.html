Bill of materials

Everything needed for two keyboards, with spares, and where to get it in India or from abroad. Prices are what the listings showed on 19 Oct 2026, or rough guides where marked with ~. Back to the build plan.

Buying strategy: one LCSC or AliExpress order for the SK6812MINI-E LEDs (not stocked in India), StacksKB for sockets and diodes, Robu.in or Lamington Road for batteries and small parts, and Lion Circuits (Bengaluru) for the boards. Batteries rarely ship to India from abroad, so buy those locally.
